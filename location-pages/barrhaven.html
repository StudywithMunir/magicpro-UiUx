Common Questions

Expert Answers for Barrhaven Residents

How frequently should I schedule property maintenance in our area?
▼
Given our region's specific climate, we highly recommend servicing your property twice a year. Scheduling a visit in the spring removes harsh winter salt and road grime, while a late-fall appointment ensures your drainage systems are completely clear of autumn leaves before freezing temperatures cause ice dams.
Are your cleaning methods safe for my landscaping and pets?
▼
Absolutely. We utilize eco-friendly, biodegradable solutions that are completely safe for your grass, garden beds, and family pets. Our technicians are trained to respect your property limits, protect fragile shrubs during ladder placement, and leave the area cleaner than we found it.
Do I need to be home during the service?
▼
For property-only jobs (like eavestrough clearing or outside glass washing), you do not need to be home. As long as we have clear access to the perimeter of your house and the property water supply, we can complete the work efficiently and send you the invoice electronically. For any interior work, an adult must be present.
Do you carry liability insurance?
▼
Yes, MagicPro is fully licensed, carries comprehensive liability insurance, and all our crew members are covered by WSIB. This protects you entirely from any liability while we are working on your property. We are happy to provide proof of insurance upon request.
