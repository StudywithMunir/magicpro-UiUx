Frequently Asked Questions

Can I perform vinyl siding cleaning Ottawa myself?
▼
Yes! Vinyl siding usually cleans up easily with nothing more than a homemade vinyl siding cleaner. For basic vinyl siding wash cleaning, use a general-purpose cleaner (like Dawn Dish Soap or Soft Scrub) mixed with a bucket full of warm water and a large sponge. A yearly scrubbing will keep it looking new! But for difficult upper-stories or extreme mold removal, we suggest professional help.
