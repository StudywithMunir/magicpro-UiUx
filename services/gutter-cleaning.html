Why Routine Gutter Cleaning is Vital

Since most people can’t visibly see their gutters, they often don’t know when they are clogged until water damage occurs. Keeping your eavestroughs clean is a very important part of making sure your home has all the cleaning services Ottawa area residents expect for total protection from weather and the elements.

If neglected, gutters can become so full of debris that it leads to overflows, which can damage your roof, siding, and even foundation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need eavestrough cleaning Ottawa services?
▼
If you see water spilling over the edges during rainfall, plant growth in your gutters, or visible sagging, you likely have a clog. However, because you cannot easily see inside them, we strongly recommend a routine flush and inspection.
Does your service include unblocking downspouts?
▼
Yes. Downspout cleaning Ottawa is a critical step in our Deluxe Gutter Cleaning package. We thoroughly flush the system to verify that water is properly flowing away from your foundation.
How often should residential gutter cleaning Ottawa take place?
▼
For most homes, twice a year is ideal—typically in the late spring and late fall. If you live surrounded by heavy tree cover, you may require more frequent cleanings.
